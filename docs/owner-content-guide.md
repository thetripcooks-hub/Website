# Trip Cooks — Content Management Guide

*Your step-by-step guide to keeping the website up to date*

---

## Getting Started

The Trip Cooks website is connected to a content management system called **Contentful**. Think of it as the control panel for your website — you can add, edit, and update content here and it automatically appears on the live site within a minute.

**To log in:**
1. Go to [app.contentful.com](https://app.contentful.com)
2. Sign in with your account
3. Select the **tripcooks-website** space from your dashboard

Once inside, click **Content** in the top navigation bar. This is where all your content lives.

---

## 1. Updating an Existing Trip

Every trip you run is listed here. To make changes:

1. Click **Content** in the top menu
2. In the content type filter on the left, select **Trip**
3. Click on the trip you want to edit (e.g. "Montenegro 2024")

You will see all the fields for that trip. Here are the ones you can now update:

---

### Group Size

This is the number of people on the trip. It appears on the featured trip card on the homepage.

- Find the **Group Size** field
- Type the group range, for example: `12–18 people` or `8–12 people`
- If you leave this empty, the website will show `12–18 people` as a default

---

### What's Not Included

This is the list of items that are **not** covered by the trip price (e.g. flights, breakfast). By default, the website always shows:

- Flight ticket
- Breakfast
- Airport pickup and transfer

If a specific trip has a **different** "not included" list, you can override it here. Click the **What's Not Included** field and add your custom items. If you leave it empty, the three defaults above will show automatically.

> **Tip:** Only fill this in if the trip differs from the standard. Leave it empty for most trips.

---

### Saving & Publishing

After making your changes:

1. Click the green **Publish** button in the top-right corner
2. The website will update within 60 seconds

> If you want to save your work without it going live yet, click **Save** instead of **Publish**. You can come back and publish later.

---

## 2. Writing a Blog Post

Blog posts are the most important tool for growing Trip Cooks on Google. Each post you publish helps new customers find you.

### Creating a New Post

1. Click **Content** in the top menu
2. Click the **Add entry** button (top right)
3. Select **Blog Post** from the list

You will see a form with the following fields:

---

**Title**
The headline of your article. Make it clear and descriptive.
*Example:* `5 Reasons Montenegro Should Be Your Next Group Trip`

---

**Slug**
This is the web address for the article. It is **auto-generated** from your title — you do not need to type it yourself. It will look like: `5-reasons-montenegro-should-be-your-next-group-trip`

> Do not change the slug once the post is published, as it will break any links people have saved.

---

**Category**
Choose one of the following:
- **Travel updates** — destination guides, trip recaps, what to expect
- **Company updates** — news about Trip Cooks, announcements
- **Support** — booking help, FAQs, practical travel advice
- **Stories from our community** — personal travel stories from your guests

---

**Author**
Your name or the name of whoever wrote the article.
*Example:* `The Trip Cooks Team` or `Amara Diallo`

---

**Date**
The publish date of the article. This is used to sort posts from newest to oldest.

---

**Excerpt**
A short summary of the article (2–3 sentences). This appears on the blog listing page and in Google search results — make it compelling.

---

**Cover Image**
The main photo for the article. Click **Add media**, then either upload a new photo or choose one from your media library.

> **Recommended size:** At least 1200 × 630 pixels. Use a high-quality photo that represents the article topic.

---

**Body**
This is the full article content. You can:
- Type and format text (bold, italic, headings)
- Add bullet points and numbered lists
- Insert images within the article
- Add quotes

The website automatically calculates how long the article takes to read and shows this to visitors.

---

### Publishing Your Post

When you are happy with everything:
1. Click **Publish** in the top-right corner
2. Your post will appear on the blog within 60 seconds

---

## 3. Adding a Community Story

Community stories are the personal accounts from your travellers — their experiences, highlights, and memories from trips with Trip Cooks. These appear on the **Community** page.

### Creating a New Story

1. Click **Content** in the top menu
2. Click **Add entry**
3. Select **Community Story**

Fill in the following fields:

---

**Title**
The headline for this story.
*Example:* `How Montenegro Changed the Way I See Travel`

---

**Slug**
Auto-generated from the title. Leave it as is.

---

**Author**
The traveller's name (with their permission).
*Example:* `Amara Diallo`

---

**Date**
The date the story was submitted or published.

---

**Read Time**
How long the story takes to read. Type this manually.
*Example:* `3 min read`

---

**Excerpt**
A short 2–3 sentence summary of the story. This is what visitors read on the community page before clicking through.

---

**Image**
A photo from the traveller's trip. Upload it or choose from your media library.

---

**Category**
Optional. You can tag the story with a category if needed.

---

**Country**
The traveller's home country.
*Example:* `Nigeria` or `Ghana`

---

**Featured**
This is a simple **on/off toggle**. Turn it **on** for the 5 stories you want shown as photo cards at the top of the Community page (the hero section). Only mark up to 5 stories as featured at a time — the most recent 5 featured stories will be shown.

> **Tip:** Choose featured stories from travellers with great photos and diverse countries — it makes the hero section look vibrant.

---

### Publishing the Story

Click **Publish** when ready. It will appear on the community page within 60 seconds.

---

## 4. Updating Reviews

Reviews from your guests appear in the *"People actually really like us"* section on the community page and homepage.

To edit a review:

1. Go to **Content**
2. Filter by **Our Wall of Love**
3. Click on the review you want to update

You can now fill in two new fields:

---

**Reviewer Name**
The first name (or full name) of the person who left the review.
*Example:* `Amara` or `Kezia M.`

If you leave this blank, no name will be shown — the review will just display the quote and trip name.

---

**Social Handle**
The reviewer's Instagram or TikTok handle (optional). If provided, it will appear below their name on the homepage review cards.
*Example:* `@amara.diallo`

---

## 5. Quick Reference

| What you want to do | Where to go in Contentful |
|---|---|
| Update a trip's group size or not-included items | Content → Trip → select the trip |
| Write a new blog article | Content → Add entry → Blog Post |
| Add a community traveller story | Content → Add entry → Community Story |
| Feature a traveller in the hero section | Community Story → turn on **Featured** toggle |
| Add a reviewer's name to a review | Content → Our Wall of Love → select the review |
| Add a social handle to a review | Content → Our Wall of Love → **Social Handle** field |

---

## Tips for Great Content

- **Publish blog posts regularly** — even one post per month makes a significant difference for Google ranking
- **Use real photos** — images from actual trips perform far better than stock photography
- **Write excerpts carefully** — they appear in Google search results and are often the reason someone clicks through
- **Feature diverse travellers** — the community hero section represents your brand. Show a range of countries and backgrounds
- **Keep titles clear** — visitors should know exactly what they're getting before they click
